## Executive Proposition

Coaching Partner call review is an end-to-end judgment workflow, not simply the act of listening to a call. A reviewer must understand why the review exists, select relevant evidence, distinguish observation from interpretation, decide whether the evidence is sufficient, identify the most useful intervention, document the work appropriately, and later determine whether the issue is resolved.

I have reconstructed that workflow from current practice and developed a bounded V1 concept that makes the process and its difficult judgment points more explicit while preserving Coaching Partner discretion.

The proposed V1 is:

> **A process-guided judgment scaffold for completing one Coaching Partner call-review workflow from initial need through follow-up and resolution.**

## V1 Workflow

**Scope -> Build Review Set -> Review Each Call (repeat) -> Synthesize -> Coach / Confirm / Commit -> Follow Up / Resolve**

The Field Guide would help a Coaching Partner answer seven practical questions:

1. Why am I reviewing?
2. What evidence should I inspect?
3. What actually happened?
4. What does the evidence collectively support?
5. Is the evidence sufficient to act?
6. What action or coaching priority matters most?
7. What later evidence would establish resolution?

## Deliberate V1 Boundaries

V1 is not:

- a QA scorecard or employee-rating system;
- a fixed-number call-sampling procedure;
- a replacement for Coaching Partner judgment;
- a claim that practices are inconsistent across the enterprise;
- a new standalone workflow system;
- an AI scoring or automated diagnosis product;
- a claim of validated demand, time savings, KPI improvement, compliance impact, or ROI;
- a request for enterprise-wide adoption.

SharePoint, Copilot, transcript-layer interaction, and PHUB integration are possible future delivery mechanisms. They are not prerequisites for validating the Field Guide's underlying workflow and utility.

## Proposed Supported Validation

The next phase would test the smallest meaningful product with leadership-approved participants and subject-matter support.

### Practitioner validation

- Determine whether another competent Coaching Partner can use the guide without Nate explaining the method beside them.
- Test whether the workflow helps users distinguish evidence, interpretation, priority, and action.
- Identify where the guide clarifies judgment and where it creates unnecessary administrative drag.
- Test the procedure against realistic, appropriately sanitized call-review examples.

### Enterprise review

- Confirm which call-selection, documentation, PHUB, escalation, follow-up, and closure expectations are authoritative.
- Identify where guidance must remain flexible rather than standardized.
- Confirm appropriate information, privacy, and enterprise-environment boundaries.

### Product validation

- Determine whether a process-guided judgment scaffold is the right product form.
- Establish which guidance belongs in the core workflow and which belongs at the point of need.
- Decide whether further prototype development is justified before considering a delivery platform.

## Success Standard for the Validation Phase

The phase succeeds if leadership can make an evidence-based decision about continued development and the team can determine whether the V1 method is usable, operationally appropriate, and worth refining.

The product-level success standard remains:

> **Another competent Coaching Partner can complete a call review from initial need to resolution without requiring Nate's personal explanation.**
