## Why This Record Exists

Mandatory enterprise AI training and supporting internal guidance were reviewed as a baseline for what the organization already teaches, permits, expects, and governs. The purpose of this record is not to archive enterprise content. It is to preserve the design consequences for the V1 Coaching Partner Call-Review Field Guide.

## Enterprise-Aligned Findings That Matter to V1

1. **Human accountability remains primary.** AI may assist work, but the employee remains responsible for reviewing outputs, applying professional judgment, and owning the outcome.
2. **AI assistance must stay inside approved tool and data boundaries.** V1 should never imply that any AI tool may receive enterprise data merely because the workflow could benefit from AI.
3. **Personal-productivity assistance is materially different from automated decision-making.** Drafting, summarizing, organizing, extracting, researching, and workflow support can fit a human-led productivity model. Automated evaluation, consequential decisions, formalized autonomous processes, or production systems can trigger additional governance requirements.
4. **Review and validation are non-optional.** AI-generated material is a starting point to inspect and refine, not evidence of correctness.
5. **The enterprise capability model emphasizes iterative use, analytical thinking, responsible AI, data-driven decisions, creative problem solving, collaboration, and human-centered design.** V1 can reinforce these capabilities without becoming an AI-training product.

## V1 Design Consequences — Adopted

### 1. Preserve the human reviewer as decision owner
AI-assisted mechanisms may help a Coaching Partner:

- organize authorized source material;
- summarize or structure notes;
- surface potentially relevant evidence;
- compare observations;
- identify unanswered questions;
- prepare coaching materials or follow-up plans.

They must not be represented as the authority that determines:

- whether an employee has a performance problem;
- the final interpretation of ambiguous evidence;
- whether formal action is warranted;
- the final coaching priority;
- whether a consequential performance decision should be made.

### 2. Keep the Field Guide fully human-operable
The V1 remains a **process-guided judgment scaffold**, not an AI-dependent workflow. If AI assistance is removed, the reviewer must still be able to complete the full method.

### 3. Treat AI as point-of-need support, not a new workflow stage
The workflow spine remains:

**Scope → Build Review Set → Review Each Call ↻ → Synthesize → Coach / Confirm / Commit → Follow Up / Resolve**

AI support may appear at selected touchpoints, but it does not become a separate user-facing stage.

### 4. Strengthen the evidence/judgment separation
Enterprise emphasis on review and human accountability reinforces the research-supported V1 discipline:

**Observable evidence → Finding → Interpretation → Sufficiency decision → Action**

AI may assist the movement of information through this path, but it does not collapse evidence into judgment.

### 5. Add a governance escalation boundary to future AI concepts
Any future concept that moves from personal productivity or decision support toward automated evaluation, formal scoring, autonomous workflow execution, production integration, or employee-impacting decisions must be treated as a separate governed use case rather than silently incorporated into V1.

## Convergence With Existing V1 Research

This enterprise baseline independently reinforces the architecture already supported by the call-review methodology research:

- structure evidence collection more strongly than diagnosis;
- preserve professional judgment at consequential decision points;
- make evidence traceable;
- require explicit sufficiency and uncertainty handling;
- avoid premature AI scoring or automated diagnosis;
- keep the product smaller than a full QA or automated decision system.

This is convergence, not proof that every proposed V1 mechanism is enterprise-approved.

## What Is Not Yet Verified

The captured enterprise baseline does **not** by itself establish:

- whether every Coaching Partner call-review artifact is permitted as an input to a specific AI tool;
- whether transcripts, performance metrics, coaching records, or other role-specific materials have additional handling restrictions;
- whether business-unit guidance narrows the general personal-productivity boundary;
- what retention or recordkeeping rules apply to AI-assisted call-review work;
- whether any future prototype that affects employee evaluation would require formal review before testing.

These questions require enterprise verification before operational deployment.
